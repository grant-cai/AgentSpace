**Importance:** high

#### Q34: Some tutors are very directive and tell students what to fix. Others ask Socratic questions. Where do you fall on that spectrum and why?
**Topics:** #methodology #socratic-method #teaching-philosophy
**Importance:** high

**Answer:**
Guide people, don't direct. Unless you are solely maximizing points for one particular subject in one particular test. It won't work for brilliant minds though, and it is very lackluster. The skills are also not too transferable to another environment.

#### Q35: Walk me through a typical first session with a new student. What do you do? What do you ask?
**Topics:** #session-structure #first-contact

**Answer:**

#### Q36: When a student shows you some workings, what's your process? What do you look for first?
**Topics:** #assessment #process
**Importance:** high

#### Q37: What questions do you typically ask students about math?

**Importance:** high

**Answer:**

#### Q38: How do you diagnose math problems? What are the common issues you see?
**Topics:** #common-problems #diagnosis #evidence
**Importance:** high

**Answer:**
